# Hybrid V1 Implementation Plan

## Goal and Scope

按Hybrid V1 Delta替换默认产品proof关键路径。本轮只实施H1，Native Codex primary；保留foreign edits和HC4中断checkpoint。

## Owners and Milestones

- **H1 — Vision semantic infrastructure**：packet owner复用M1代表帧/PNG，schema owner严格response和ID/group绑定，router owner复用AgentProvider/ModelConnections，不复制HTTP/key/catalog。有限source session、roles、receipt、capability audit、controlled tests和one-call development diagnostic。PRODUCT_DISABLED；不生成mask或导出。
- **H2 — Candidate confirmation/grouping**：接入真实候选集合与source范围，处理Luna/Sol语义并建立logical groups；同packet有限comparison与product-print/subtitle false-cover统计。
- **H3 — Motion/shape/coverage**：16–32采样motion heuristic、异常→Sol，原conservative mask及multi-component extraction，复用alpha/candidates并硬核100% opaque coverage；small component不依赖strict proof。
- **H4 — Preview/backend E2E**：真实原片/覆盖成片成对start/middle/end/anomaly，MiniMax独立QA与有界Sol二审；233s及controlled静态/multi/move/disappear/product/subtitle/padding验证，再讨论activation。

## Compatibility and Verification

H1新增development模块，不改旧proof/store/schema/consumer/queue/guard。只为现有provider增加受控structured vision调用接缝。typecheck、新tests、现有provider/router/packet/parser回归、实际可用route一次diagnostic、owned Harness、AOCI维护、Parent Risk Gate、单slice commit/push/核远端。旧HC4证据单独commit，不能混作H1验收。

## Self Review

当前catalog缺最初指定的gpt-6-luna/gpt-6.1-sol；用户已明确授权改用catalog中的gpt-5.6-luna/gpt-5.6-sol。MiniMax ID来自app配置，当前MiniMax-M3；必须实际image probe确认，不能仅借名称猜权限。H1 infrastructure可以支持logical route，但真实routing评价缺席时必须报告未评估。下一slice只有H2；缺capability或必需验证不能伪报READY。
